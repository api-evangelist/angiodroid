On February 12th, 2026, the French Senate hosted the press conference “Patient Safety and Responsible Innovation in Healthcare: CO 2 in Angiography, Preserving Renal Function” , promoted by Senator Alain Milon with the contribution of several scientific and healthcare associations: Handicap République et Société (HRS), Société de Chirurgie Vasculaire et Endovasculaire (SCVE) and the National Federation of Radiologist Physicians (FNMR). In the sake of our mission to #SaveTheKidneys, it was a great honour for us to partake in this important event with our non-conditional presence .

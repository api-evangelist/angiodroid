On October 29th , 2025, Angiodroid was pleased to take part in the 5th Edition of the Healthcare Innovation Forum , the key event for analyzing and discussing the most advanced strategies for the transformation of the Italian healthcare system . The event was held in the prestigious setting of Palazzo Wedekind in Rome , gathering institutional representatives, healthcare managers, innovation experts, and key stakeholders in the sector.
